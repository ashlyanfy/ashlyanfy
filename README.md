## About Me

I am a second-year Software Engineering student with a broad interest in information technology — from frontend and backend development to full-stack architecture and machine learning. Rather than locking myself into a single specialization early, I deliberately explore different areas of the field to understand how modern systems are designed, built, and connected end to end.

Most of my experience comes from practice, not coursework. I build projects for real work tasks, develop my own startup ideas, and regularly take part in hackathons, where tight deadlines force clear thinking, fast decision-making, and working code over perfect code. I believe this is the fastest way to grow as an engineer: shipping things that real people actually use.

My long-term ambition goes beyond software. I plan to move toward robotics engineering or astrophysics — fields where code meets the physical world. Software is my foundation; building systems that sense, move, and interact with reality is the goal.

## Current Focus

- Strengthening my core skills in **Java** — object-oriented design, collections, and building reliable applications
- Getting started with **AWS** and cloud infrastructure — deployment, storage, and the services behind scalable systems
- Improving my engineering fundamentals through real projects: work assignments, startup prototypes, and hackathon builds
